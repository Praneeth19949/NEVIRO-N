# NEVIRO — Money • Budget • Fuel (Locked V1)

Countries: Australia, Sri Lanka, India, New Zealand, Nepal, Singapore.

- First launch: country + currency selection; editable later.
- Local-first: no required account; money records remain on device.
- Dashboard: income, expenses, balance, budgets, recent transactions.
- Daily expense categories with drill-down, add and delete.
- Income: salary, side/business, other.
- Monthly category budgets.
- Fuel fill-ups automatically count as Fuel expenses.
- Receipt scan: camera/gallery OCR with user review before save. ML Kit Android release dependencies are patched; real-device testing is still required before calling the scan bug fully verified.
- Australia: preserve FuelWatch experience and increase nearby radius from 10 km to 20 km.
- Other countries: only free, reliable and legally usable fuel data; otherwise reference/manual tracking. Never fabricate live prices.
- Reports: income, expenses, balance, category breakdown, fuel summary.
- Excel export/share: All Transactions, Expenses, Income, Fuel Records and Summary sheets.
- Brand direction: navy/blue + green/teal + white cards.
- Android first; iOS after Android is stable.
