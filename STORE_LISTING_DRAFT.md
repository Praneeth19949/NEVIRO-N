# NEVIRO Store Listing Draft

## App name
NEVIRO

## Subtitle / short description
Fuel Tracker & Fuel Price Finder

## Short description
Track fuel costs, compare supported fuel prices, log odometer readings and understand your driving expenses.

## Full description draft
NEVIRO helps drivers keep fuel spending simple. Record every fill-up, track litres and cost per litre, add odometer readings, review weekly and monthly trends, and export your records to Excel.

In supported locations, NEVIRO can also show current fuel-price information so you can compare nearby stations before you fill up. Western Australia uses official FuelWatch data. Other regions are enabled only when a suitable data source is available.

Key features:
- Fuel expense tracking
- Automatic total calculation
- Optional odometer readings
- Fuel efficiency estimates
- Weekly, monthly and yearly reports
- Fuel history with edit/delete
- Excel export
- Automatic region and currency defaults
- Nearby price comparison in supported regions
- Directions to selected stations
- No account required in Version 1
